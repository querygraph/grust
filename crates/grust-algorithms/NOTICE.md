# Source provenance

The generalized analytics work was informed by the Grustcat and Icecat Rust
implementations at querygraph/icecat commit
`3cbc07a510c0bf1803f83a10776d40d0e6f556ba`, including packed adjacency, iterative
components, shortest-path reconstruction and weighted PageRank conventions.
The upstream MIT license and copyright notices are retained in `LICENSE`.
